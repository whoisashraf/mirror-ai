# Mirror AI — 90-second demo script

Use `VITE_DEMO_MODE=true` for a deterministic UI rehearsal, or the configured Gemini backend for the real generation demo.

1. Open `/store/mirror-atelier?demo=1` and show that the experience is branded as the merchant, not a marketplace.
2. Open **Navy Oversized Blazer** and tap **Try it on**.
3. Accept photo consent and choose **Use demo model** (or upload the known-good full-body test photo).
4. Show the preflight checks, then continue into the fitting room.
5. Point out that the generated/reference image remains visible while the stylist conversation scrolls independently.
6. Ask: **Can I wear this to an interview?**
7. Ask: **What should I pair with it?**
8. Tap **Add to look** on **Cream Satin Blouse**, **Black Tailored Trousers**, then **Black Leather Loafers**. Each update becomes a new look while keeping prior looks in history.
9. Ask the stylist to remove/swap an item and use the structured action button it returns.
10. Open **Shop look** to show the merchant-only product list and total.
11. Open `/dashboard` and show funnel, shopper question themes and products generating interest.

Before another pitch, use **Demo tools → Reset demo**.

## Never claim

- that the generated image guarantees physical fit;
- that a checkout click is a completed purchase;
- that Mirror has proven return reduction or conversion lift before the pilot measures it;
- that a deterministic demo fallback is real AI generation.
